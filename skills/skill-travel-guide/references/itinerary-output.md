# Itinerary Output

Use the smallest format that still lets the traveler act without reconstructing the plan.

## Standard Structure

### 1. Trip Card

State destination, dates, traveler profile, arrival/departure anchors, lodging area, accepted transport, priorities, exclusions, and the research cutoff date.

### 2. Decision Summary

Explain the route theme, why days are grouped this way, and which two or three experiences receive the most time. Call out the first items to remove under pressure.

### 3. Daily Timeline

Use one heading per day and time blocks in this shape:

```text
08:30–09:10 | Hotel -> attraction | taxi, about 40 min plus pickup buffer
09:15–12:30 | Attraction | core
Purpose: ...
Order and highlights: ...
Technique: ...
Time short: keep ...; skip ...
Leave by: ...
```

Include meals in the timeline, then provide fuller evidence in the food table. Avoid impossible precision; use ranges when uncertainty is real.

### 4. Food Table

Use the exact fields from `food-evidence.md`. Make the small-eatery/formal-restaurant balance visible with labels.

### 5. Plan B Matrix

| Trigger | Replacement | Transport/reservation change | Drop first | Decision deadline |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Distinguish ordinary rain from severe-weather warnings. A useful Plan B says when to switch; a list of indoor attractions is not enough.

### 6. Final Checks

Group reminders by:

- reservation and identification;
- transport tickets, shuttle inventory, and pickup points;
- weather, clothing, footwear, sun/rain protection;
- photography, heritage protection, religious or local etiquette;
- diet, allergies, medicine, hydration, and food portions;
- luggage storage, checkout, and departure buffer.

### 7. Sources

Group links by `official`, `map/transport`, `weather`, and `food evidence`. Put a checked date beside dynamic sources.

## Detail Controls

- `Quick answer`: route summary, key transport, two food tracks, Plan B, and critical cautions.
- `Final guide`: full timelines, attraction tactics, food evidence, reservations, and risk matrix.
- `All details`: add photography, accessibility, fatigue rules, ordering quantities, and decision thresholds.

Do not make every stop equally verbose. Match detail to priority.

## Uncertainty Language

Use precise labels:

- `已核实 YYYY-MM-DD`（替换为实际核实日期）
- `预测，出发前 72 小时复核`
- `地图估时，不含极端拥堵`
- `仅作发现线索，不作为本地口碑结论`
- `证据不足，建议到店前电话确认`

Avoid a single blanket disclaimer at the end. Put uncertainty next to the decision it affects.
